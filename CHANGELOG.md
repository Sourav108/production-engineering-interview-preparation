## [v2.0.0] - 2026-09-03

### Architectural & Structural Enhancements
- **Consolidated Operating Principles (§5)**: Unified all anti-fabrication, currency verification, original text, and lab safety guidelines into a single authoritative section.
- **Streamlined Module Specifications (§9)**: Replaced verbose repetitive module boilerplate with high-density, outcome-oriented specifications.
- **Rigorous Definition of Done (§8, §18)**: Defined precise DoD criteria for lessons, incident scenarios, runbooks, interview questions, postmortems, labs, projects, and overall repository completion.
- **Pre-Commit Self-Review Checklist (§18.2)**: Established automated and manual validation rules to prevent hallucinations and stale claims.
- **Diagramming Standard (§6)**: Enforced GitHub-native Mermaid diagrams for branching and multi-node flows while preserving ASCII for linear pipelines.
- **Sibling Boundary & References (§3)**: Added `REFERENCES.md` to cleanly delineate production engineering from adjacent repositories (Java, Spring Boot, SQL, Cloud, Kubernetes).
- **Session Continuity Protocol (§17)**: Added explicit filesystem verification commands to prevent state drift during incremental execution sessions.
- **Adjustable Question Bank Cadence (§10)**: Standardized interview question generation into 20 batches of 25 questions each (500 target).
