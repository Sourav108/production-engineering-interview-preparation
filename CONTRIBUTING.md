# Contributing Guide

Guidelines for contributing lessons, labs, runbooks, and interview questions to `production-engineering-interview-preparation`.

---

## 📋 Contribution Standards

1. **Adhere to the Definition of Done**: Every new lesson, incident, runbook, or lab must follow its corresponding template in [`templates/`](templates/).
2. **Zero Fabrication**: Never invent fake metrics, benchmark charts, or imaginary incident outputs.
3. **Safety First**: Labs and failure injection exercises must operate in isolated local containers.
4. **Originality**: All content must be original and explain concepts from first principles.
