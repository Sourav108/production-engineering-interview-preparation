# Production Postmortem Templates & Guides

A guide to conducting blameless postmortems and turning production incidents into permanent systemic reliability improvements.

---

## 🏛️ Principles of Blameless Postmortems
1. **Focus on Systems, Not People**: Human error is a symptom of poorly designed tools, confusing interfaces, and inadequate guardrails.
2. **Detailed Timeline Discipline**: Reconstruct the exact sequence of events in UTC.
3. **Actionable Prevention Items**: Every identified contributing factor must map to an action item with an assigned owner and due date.
